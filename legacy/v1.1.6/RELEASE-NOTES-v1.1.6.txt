VMM PO Governance - v1.1.6  (4 Nov 2026)  - for 10.10.16.35
==========================================================

Built on v1.1.4 (3.1.4), the version installed on .35. Users, passwords, data, history and
config.json are kept. No database change (same files), so going back to v1.1.4 is safe at any time.
Multi concept (True Repeat, BOBs, new options) and the multi concept Recommendation are NOT changed.
No workflow change: Watch List still goes to the Category Head under Approvals, as today.

SINGLE CONCEPT - CALCULATION CHANGES (agreed 4 Nov 2026)
--------------------------------------------------------
1. Late PO (PO Assessment). From the 1st to the cut-off day (default 5), a pending PO of last
   month is counted as stock at the window start (1st of this month), not as a PO landing inside
   its own window. Only the first month of the roll changes. Note added to the reason:
   "[Includes X units of previous month's (MM.YYYY) pending PO, counted in the stock at the window
   start (MM.YYYY) - Day-of-month cut-off = N.]"
   Example (29 Oct data, assessed 4 Nov): PO 5901123971, 1130156584 BLK, Oct 2026, 8,400 pcs:
   v1.1.4 Retain (STR 54 days) -> v1.1.6 Reduce Qty, keep 490 (STR 926 days).
2. No F4 plan (D1). No plan for the Brand x MC x MRP x Flag in the requirement window (D days
   from the 1st of the month after delivery):
   - New PO request: Hold - "No Plan Available ... Ask the planning team to add the plan."
     (was Auto Allow, no limit). Checked first.
   - PO Assessment: new status "No Plan - Review" (was Retain). No quantity is suggested; the
     planner decides. Own KPI tile, own "No Plan" filter button, own colour in the Excel export.
     Not counted in Qty / Value at Risk.
3. Days left in the month count today (D2). F1 stock is yesterday's close, so today's sale is still
   to come. On 4 Nov (30-day month) 27 days are counted instead of 26. New PO request, single
   concept Recommendation and PO Assessment use the same rule. Effect: about one more day of sale
   before the window start, so Max Permissible goes up by about one day's sale of the colour
   (LGY Dec 2026 on 4 Nov: 9,064 -> 9,222).
4. Colour with no sale (D3). A colour share under 2% already gave Watch List. New: when the MC has
   no sale at all in F3 (the share cannot be worked out) the New PO request gives Watch List
   (was Auto Allow "No forecast data").
5. Article not in the Article Master -> the New PO request stops (single and multi concept),
   with a message; nothing is saved. THE ARTICLE MASTER MUST BE UPLOADED (it was optional): without
   it every New PO request stops with "The Article Master is not uploaded".

SINGLE CONCEPT - SCREENS (display only, no number changes)
----------------------------------------------------------
6. Caution note on the New PO request (Allow only): when, with this request, a later PO will be
   1,000 pcs or more too much on a later 1st, the card names it, e.g. "the Feb 2027 PO 5,000 will
   be 1,963 too much on 1 Mar 2027 (118d vs 79d limit). Consider reducing it to 3,037."
   Step 6 of the Step-by-Step Computation shows the working. Information only.
7. Step 4 heading: "STR Calculation - 75-Day Window from Day 1 of the Month after Delivery"
   (was "60-Day Window from Day 1 of Delivery Month"; the calculation was already right).
8. "Why introduce this colour?" - single concept Watch List (colour under 2% or no sale): the buyer
   must type the reason before the request is saved and sent to the Category Head. "Don't send"
   saves nothing. The approver sees the reason in Approvals; it is also on the result card.
9. Generic Article description (grey) and vendor under the code on the result card and in
   Today's Requests.
10. "How was this number worked out?" side panel: numbered steps in plain English with an Excel
    download (formulas) - on the single concept New PO request card and on every quantity / Hold /
    Watch List of the single concept Recommendation (+ "Download Excel (all lines)").
11. Parameters panel: "Late PO counts until day" (default 5, whole days 1-28). It was fixed at 5
    in the program with no screen.
