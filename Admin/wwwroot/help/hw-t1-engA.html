2
BOBs — Parent Style Check (only for BOBs option type)
Before allowing a new colour, check if the parent style is a healthy seller
For BOBs, we look at all the other colours of the option (same GM) in the BSR and keep those with WOF ≥ 4 weeks. Each gets its band in its peer group (Brand × MC × MRP × Flag) by per-day STR%; the worst band decides.

Every other colour in the top 20% (Allow band): Allow when the oldest other colour (most weeks on floor) also meets the minimum of the option's Entry / Mid / Fashion type; below it → Block; MC-MRP not in the mapping → Hold.
Any other colour in the 20–30% band (none below): Exception — the buyer may raise it to the B&M Head with a reason (MRA Level 8).
Any other colour below the top 30%: Block.
Other colours exist but none has 4 weeks on floor, or no other colours at all: Auto Allow, as before.

3
BSR Reference — Which data to rank against?
CY BSR first — LY SS+AW combined if CY pool is too thin
Primary: CY BSR (SS26) — current season data.
Peer group: Brand × MC × MRP × Flag.
Fallback: If CY peer group has fewer than 5 qualifying styles (after WOF ≥ 4w filter), system merges LY BSR SS25 + LY BSR AW25 into one combined pool.

Decision reason always states the data source — e.g. "64th pct (Below 30%) in peer group of 21 styles (Fallback BSR (SS25+AW25))".
Example: MC 113030008, MRP ₹79, Normal — only 1 style in CY BSR (WOF=9w). Peer min=5 not met → fallback season BSR used → 21 styles available.
4
Peer Ranking — Filter → Rank → Percentile Band
1
Filter — WOF ≥ 4 weeks
Only articles with at least 4 weeks on floor are included in peer pool. If your article itself has WOF < 4w → Auto Allow (insufficient history).
2
Rank — Per-Day Sell-Through %
Per-Day STR% = Total Sell-Through % across 3 weeks ÷ 21 days. Normalises for time — directly comparable regardless of when the style launched. Full WOF-filtered pool used — no IQR removal.
3
Percentile Band — Where does the option land?
Position = share of the peer group selling at or below the option.
Top 20% (above the 80th percentile) → Allow when it also sells at least the minimum of its Entry / Mid / Fashion type (first 3 weeks): Entry 2.0%, Mid 1.5%, Fashion 1.5% per day — below it → Block; MC-MRP not in the mapping → Hold “Planner has not maintained this MC-MRP in the master.”
20–30% (above the 70th, up to the 80th percentile) → Exception: the buyer may raise an exception at MRA Level 8 with a reason; the B&M Head approves or rejects it. No minimum check.
Below the top 30% → Block. No Watch List and no border zone for multi concept.
Example (True Repeat): MC=112010016, MRP ₹699, Normal, brand NON-PL · 28 NON-PL styles pass WOF≥4w. At 2.40%/day, 18 of 28 sell at or below = 64th percentile → below the top 30% → Block. At 2.90%/day, 21 of 28 = 75th percentile → 20–30% → Exception (B&M Head). At 3.10%/day, 23 of 28 = 82nd percentile → top 20% → Allow if it meets its Entry / Mid / Fashion minimum.
🚨 Absolute Block Override — fires in any band
Rule: If Per-Day STR% < 1%/day AND Weeks on Floor ≥ 4 weeks → Block immediately, even in the top 20%.

Why: A percentile band is relative — an article can be in the top 20% of a category where all articles are slow. The Absolute Block is an absolute threshold that catches any article selling at less than 1% of stock per day after 4+ weeks on the floor. This is an extremely poor seller by any standard.

Where it applies: Both Tool 1 (before raising) and Tool 2 (on existing raised POs). Applied after the band — even a top-20% article is blocked if it breaches this threshold.
